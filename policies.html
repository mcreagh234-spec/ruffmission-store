Returns & refunds

90-day money-back guarantee. We accept eligible returns within 90 days of delivery. Items should be returned in their original condition and packaging. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible with photos of the item and packaging. We'll review the issue and arrange a replacement or refund when eligible.

To start a return, contact us with your order number. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method; your bank may take 5–10 business days to post it.
